What is the purpose of Kabankalan Eats / Food Finder system?

Kabankalan Eats was created to help the people of Kabankalan and visitors easily find delicious, affordable, and ready-to-eat food — without having to wander around the city. From Cansi, Inasal, Fast Food, Cafes, Milk Tea, to Desserts, everything is in one place.

Map & Location

🎯 What is the goal of this system?

  • 1. For students and workers — who want to eat but don't have time to search. Just search, and you'll find it right away.
  • 2. For tourists and visitors — who don't know where to eat delicious food in Kabankalan. We have ratings and reviews to guide you.
  • 3. For local businesses — so they can be seen online, even small carinderias or cafes.
  • 4. To promote Kabankalan's food — especially our famous Cansi, Inasal, and Batchoy that we are proud of.
