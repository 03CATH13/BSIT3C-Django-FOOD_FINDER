About Food Finder

Your ultimate guide to the best eats in Kabankalan City.

What is KabankalanEats?

KabankalanEats (Food Finder System) is a web-based application designed to help people—including tourists and locals—find the most delicious food spots in Kabankalan City.

Our goal is to make food discovery easy and convenient by providing a centralized platform where users can browse restaurants, view menus, check locations, and access contact details.

Key Features

  • Search for restaurants by name, cuisine, or dish.
  • View full menus and prices for each restaurant.
  • Save your favorite restaurants to your "Favorites" list.
  • Locate restaurants using Google Maps integration.
  • Register and log in to access personalized features.

About the Developer

This system was developed as a project for BSIT 3C at Kabankalan Catholic College (KCC). It is built using the Django Framework (Python), Bootstrap, and Custom CSS for a modern, dark-mode design.
